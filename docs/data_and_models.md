# Data and ML Models

## Data Assumptions
- The MVP operates on a snapshot of student data containing demographic, academic, and engagement metrics.
- Financial data and severe personal background data are avoided to minimize unnecessary sensitivity.
- We assume that `attendance_rate`, `grades`, and `lms_engagement` are updated periodically and represent rolling averages or current-semester metrics.

## Model Assumptions
- The system predicts an **elevated risk of dropout or non-enrollment**, not absolute certainty.
- Risk is classified into three tiers: LOW, MEDIUM, and HIGH to provide actionable insights for counselors.
- We assume intervention effectiveness depends on early detection, meaning risk trajectory (improving vs. degrading) is as important as the static risk score.

## Synthetic-Data Limitations
- For this hackathon MVP, the system uses synthetic (demo) data.
- The `students.csv` and `student_metrics.csv` files are generated by a script and do not reflect real individuals.
- The machine learning model (XGBoost) is trained on this synthetic data. Therefore, the risk probabilities and SHAP explanations (risk factors) reflect the synthetic distributions, not real-world validated insights. 
- *This model must not be deployed to production without retraining on real institutional data.*

## Known Limitations
- The current implementation does not ingest live data (e.g., streaming from LMS or ERP systems).
- Fairness and bias monitoring are currently out of scope for the MVP.
- The SHAP explainer uses a static background dataset to compute expected values.
